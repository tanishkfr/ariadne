## Rollback transport defect and checkpoint 4

The exact installed-product rollback exercise initially stopped when the
V1.5.1 runtime verified a V1.5.2-created S1 packet from a checkout with
different CRLF/LF bytes. The packet carried a hash of a delivered prompt
fragment in the legacy `content_sha256` field, but the rollback verifier used
that field as the newline-normalised hash of the complete canonical source.
This was a deterministic transport-compatibility defect, not a social-policy
or workflow defect.

`f443dd048a5ea3b546fd72abb57fec511a3ed951` keeps the raw source hash,
restores `content_sha256` as the backward-compatible normalised full-source
hash, and adds `delivered_content_sha256` for exact packet-section integrity.
Old manifests remain readable. A packet produced by the repaired current
runtime verifies under both current and clean V1.5.1 runtimes; changed source
content and changed delivered sections fail independently. The packet suite
passes 41/41 and the full aggregate suite passes.

## Checkpoint 1 candidate — evidence state and S2/S3 transport

**Established defect:** V1.1 could prove that a packet and output file existed,
but could not prove the difference between a recommended skill, an invoked
method, a completed output and downstream use. A URL in `DESIGN.md` or
`RESEARCH.md` could also assert its own inspection without an independent
retrieval artifact.

**Change:** Added one project-local `.builderos/creative-evidence.json` schema
owned by `scripts/creative-intelligence.py`. It selects research depth and
project-specific methods from a reasoned assessment, records strict skill-state
transitions, separates found/inspected/inaccessible references, hashes evidence
and output artifacts, records conflicts and reference-to-decision traces, and
checks external claims in `RESEARCH.md` and `DESIGN.md`.

The existing S2/S3 packet generator now carries that ledger when present and
leaves legacy runs explicitly untracked when absent. S5 receives none of it.
The Builder OS entry skill hides assessment/event mechanics from the user and
uses progressive disclosure through one focused reference file.

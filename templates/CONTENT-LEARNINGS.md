## Caveats

> Re-read before drawing any conclusion from the numbers above.

- Sample sizes are small. Most differences are noise.
- Platform algorithms change without notice and without explanation.
- Timing, day of week, and who happened to see it early dominate outcomes.
- A post's performance is mostly determined by things outside your control.
- **Never build a rule from one post.**
